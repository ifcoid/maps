    Sources & notes
    • Site corners P1–P4, existing building and the 50 kVA transformer: Inception Report (Table 3, Fig. 41–44), WGS84.
    • Road and EDTL transmission line geometry: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). The OSM road from junction A reaches the site boundary at the length shown; the Inception Report gives 9.4 km to the site.
    • Section A–C is the first 4.0 km from junction A (concrete-stabilized, IR Fig. 41); C–E is the degraded unpaved remainder.
    • The MV line alignment and the supply tie-in are indicative (not surveyed). Substation, genset and MV works are MEP/EDTL scope; they are shown here because they set the ITC power interface.
    • Master Plan Rev.14 is drawn on a local survey grid (BM1–BM3) with no geodetic tie, and every geographic coordinate in the Inception Report comes from Google Earth. The plan is placed by a rigid best fit (scale 1, rotation −2.66°) to the P1–P4 corners and the existing building: position accuracy ±50 m. Checks: the Rev.14 main gate falls 9 m from where the access road reaches the site, and all CPT/GRS field points fall inside the 25 ha parcel.
    • The P1–P4 corners (18 ha) do not match the Rev.14 parcel (25 ha) in shape; Rev.14 governs. Proposal: one GNSS session on BM1–BM3 to tie the survey grid to WGS84/UTM 51S and remove the ±50 m uncertainty.
    • ITC design layers (fiber ring, spurs, cameras, wall detection zones, security grades, DAS zones, radio) come from the ITC drawing set Rev.B of the Interim Design Rev.1 (sheets ITC-GEN-002/003, NET-002, VSS-001, PID-001, COM-002), built from the same CAD coordinates. Positions are provisional until the D-4 detailed design and carry the ±50 m placement of the plan.
    • The map shows the 66 site and perimeter cameras and the 20 cell-block cameras. The remaining cameras of the 144 (hub, isolation, support buildings, head office) are allocated per building on the schematic sheet ITC-VSS-003, which has no plan coordinates. Tower PTZ and thermal symbols are offset from their tower for legibility, as on the sheet.
    • Security grades, DAS zones and survey contours are off by default: tick them in the legend.
    • Survey contours: layer 1 KONTUR of Master Plan Rev.14, 1 m interval (index every 5 m), on the BM1–BM3 grid with the same ±50 m placement. Survey elevations are GNSS ellipsoid heights (project level ±0.000 = El. +160.00); height above mean sea level is about El. − 41.5 m (EGM2008 geoid), so the site sits at roughly +118 to +135 m MSL.
    • Map tools: fullscreen and distance/area measurement (top left), coordinates under the cursor in WGS84 and the Rev.14 CAD grid (bottom left, click to hold and copy; CAD values carry the ±50 m placement), PNG export of the current view (button above). Hover a feature for its name, click for details.
    • Basemaps (picture card at the top right of the map: point at it or tap it to see all choices, each previewed at this site): ArcGIS Hybrid (imagery with place and road labels), ArcGIS Imagery, ArcGIS Streets, ArcGIS Topographic, OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap. At this site ArcGIS imagery goes to zoom 18 and the ArcGIS street and topographic maps to zoom 17; deeper zoom enlarges those tiles.
    • OpenTopoMap (OpenStreetMap + SRTM terrain, CC-BY-SA) is for regional relief only. Its contours are coarse (SRTM 30 m) and use mean sea level; it stops at zoom 17.
    • Cell blocks: the typical Cell Block A–E plan and its 4 cameras (ITC-CELLBLOCK-OVERLAY.dwg, sheet ITC-VSS-002) are placed on each of the five MP no. 8 roofs (same 40.5 × 17.0 m outline), with the sally-port end at the entrance canopy. Left/right handedness is indicative, and block letters A–E are not given on the Master Plan.
    • Fiber and microwave routes: ITU Broadband Maps transmission map (bbmaps.itu.int), operator-reported (Timor-Leste go-live 2014), drawn with few points. Reference only, not a design input; non-commercial use with attribution under the ITU terms. The nearest node on record (“Manufahi”, Telemor) is 4.8 km from the site; its position may be a placeholder.
    • Mobile coverage: notes read from the nPerf coverage map on 28 Oct 2026 and placed at OpenStreetMap town locations. nPerf tiles and data are not reproduced, because the nPerf terms of use require written permission; each note links to the live nPerf map. nPerf is crowd-sourced: no samples is not proof of no signal.
    • Drawing annotations reproduce the labels and leaders of the six A3 site sheets from ITC-SITE-OVERLAY-REV14.dwg.
